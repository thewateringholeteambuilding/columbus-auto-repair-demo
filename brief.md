## References used

### 1. TWH: Alexander's Automotive (industrial x iron-brass)
- Hero composition: Full-bleed photo hero with gradient overlay from bottom, mechanic under hood. Text anchored bottom-left with clock icon + hours line above the headline.
- Section order: Hero > Stats strip > Services snapshot (4 of 8) > Reviews > Process timeline > FAQ accordion > Map + CTA
- Copy voice: Direct, staccato. Short declarative sentences. No adjective inflation. Specific numbers (152 reviews, 19 years, 12-hour days). Service descriptions lead with what the tech physically does.
- Photo treatment: Unsplash mechanic/garage imagery. Real-work context (under hood, on lift, diagnostic tools). No staged stock.
- Trust signals: Star rating + review count as stats strip. ASE/AAA/NAPA/E-Check certifications on About page. Specific warranty terms (24-month, 24,000-mile).
- Conversion path: "Book your appointment" CTA routes to /estimate form. Secondary "Call [phone]" on every section. Sticky mobile call bar.
- **Apply to this build:** Full-bleed hero with Ken Burns zoom, stats strip immediately after hero, dark iron palette with brass accents on square-edged cards, process timeline with numbered steps, FAQ accordion with ref-measured scrollHeight

### 2. TWH: Kaimuki Auto Repair (industrial x solar-ember)
- Hero composition: Full-bleed with value statement headline ("Honest Auto Repair. Since 1971.") paired with action mechanic shot.
- Section order: Hero > How It Works (process first before services) > Services
- Copy voice: Conversational, neighbor-to-neighbor. "No surprises. No 'while we were in there' tacks." Short staccato bursts mixed with specific vehicle references.
- Photo treatment: Documentary-style, unglamorous natural lighting, real labor. No dealership-grade aesthetics.
- Trust signals: Hyper-specific: "54 years," "same corner," founder name "Alan," customer names tied to vehicle year models, NAPA + BBB badges.
- Conversion path: CTAs land on phone-first contact. Process transparency before the ask.
- **Apply to this build:** Conversational, staccato copy voice. Specific vehicle references in testimonials. Location-anchored headline (Dublin Granville Rd, not generic Columbus). Process transparency as trust-builder.

### Cross-reference design thesis
Lead with a full-bleed hero anchored to the physical address (per both references). Keep the Iron Edge palette (dark nav, brass accent, zero radius). Vary from All Hours by: leading with a certifications bar instead of a stats strip after the hero, using a 2-column about section with shop photo, and writing Columbus-specific geography (I-270, Westerville, Northland). Copy voice stays direct and staccato with specific operational details (172 reviews, 3.7 rating contextualized honestly as a high-volume shop).

## Differentiation from All Hours Automotive (batch sibling in same niche)
